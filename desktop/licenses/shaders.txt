Screen filter shaders

melonadeDS for PC draws its screens with the Android app's shaders (app/src/main/java/me/magnum/melonds/common/opengl/ShaderProgramSource.kt),
embedded in the exe. These notices are the ones that file's comments carry, and the app lists the same in its open source licenses
(app/config/libraries/shader-*.json).

Written for melonadeDS from published algorithms
------------------------------------------------

Quilez filter: based on "Improved texture interpolation" by Iñigo Quílez
(http://www.iquilezles.org/www/articles/texture/texture.htm)
Scale2x filter: Scale2x (also known as EPX) by Andrea Mazzoleni (https://www.scale2x.it/algorithm)
FXAA filter: FXAA by Timothy Lottes (NVIDIA), the original simple version
Smooth gradients (deband): after mpv's deband (itself based on flash3kyuu), with the "Hash without Sine" function by Dave Hoskins (MIT)
