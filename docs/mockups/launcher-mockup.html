Game launcher, three ways

A design mock-up for the melonadeDS library screen. Tap a tile to open the metadata sheet. Everything is fictional and procedurally drawn.

Modes

  • List is the current look: 48 px banner icon, 18 px title, filename caption, hairline dividers.
  • Grid uses box-art cards at a 9:10 ratio (DS cases are slightly taller than wide), 3 columns on a phone. Banner icon badge bottom-left, RetroAchievements trophy top-right, publisher and year chips below.
  • Mosaic is a dense 4-column CSS grid. Recently played titles span 2×2 with a Continue button, landscape screenshot tiles span 2×1, the rest are 1×1. grid-auto-flow: dense back-fills holes.

Degraded state

Two ROMs have no RetroAchievements entry. They fall back to a grey card with the banner icon centred and the filename, so an unmatched dump still looks intentional rather than broken.

Theme

Material 3 roles (surface, surface-container, primary, on-surface, outline-variant) with a melon-green seed. Light and dark follow the system; the sun/moon button overrides.

Tip: resize the window; the phone keeps 412 px and the panel stacks below it.
