# EXPAND parity for WideMelon

Plan for bringing everything PokeDaisy's widescreen ("EXPAND") does into WideMelon, written 11-17. It replaces the PokeDaisy notes in [plan-widemelon.md](plan-widemelon.md) as the list of what's missing; that plan still owns the WideMelon design and the GPU 2D steps this one leans on. How WideMelon works today is in [MODS.md](../MODS.md).

Why: WideMelon works in every game and stays correct around display capture, but EXPAND does more for the Pokemon games: game-aware auto zoom, 2D carried into the margins, and full battle scenes. Getting to parity also makes it realistic to offer our core to PokeDaisy, since they could drop their own widescreen code (`pk_nds_wide.cpp`, 1,641 lines, and about 40% of their melonDS patch) instead of porting it onto our renderer.

PokeDaisy is GPL-3.0 like us. Code we copy keeps its credit and a note on where it came from. Their repo is at `C:\Dev\pokedaisy`.

## How the two differ

EXPAND is Pokemon-only. It turns on for carts its RAM reader knows (Platinum, HG/SS, Black/White, Black 2/White 2, several regions, plus a few hacks) and is off for everything else, for menus and the title (it drops to 4:3 with a blurred backdrop), and for link play.

- At 1x it runs on the **soft renderer**: four helper threads each own a `SoftRenderer` and draw eight slices (left, right, above, below, corners) from shifted copies of the polygon list.
- At 2x and up it uses the GL renderer with one wide polygon list and an off-axis crop of just the window.
- Margin 2D is composed on the CPU from a snapshot of engine A's registers taken at line 192.

WideMelon is any game, one pass on OpenGL or Compute (the clip-space squeeze), with no soft support (shelved). The wings get unit A's 3D and the edge column's blending and fade. 2D in the wings waits for the GPU shadow 2D renderer. Capture, `GetLine` and `Hash3D` read only the native middle, hi-res capture is wide-aware, and dual-screen games get the wing hold. Nothing in it reads game RAM.

What WideMelon keeps that EXPAND doesn't have: any game, any viewport, OpenGL and Compute in one pass, capture and hi-res capture handled, the dual-screen wing hold, wings that follow unit A to the bottom screen, per-layout ratios, and Lua camera control.

## Phases

Sizes are in lines and phone steps.

**Phase 1: game state and camera (app only, no core change).**
- Copy `pk_nds_field.cpp` and its header, the generated ports table and the one GPU read (`pk_nds_field_gpu.cpp`) behind a read-RAM interface.
- Add the scene-based auto zoom rule, the easing and the look-around follow to `WideCamera.kt`.
- Add the AUTO ZOOM IN setting, and expose the scene to Lua as `melon.game.state()`.
- Size: about 1,300 copied lines plus 300–500 new ones.
- Phone check: conversations, signs and the X menu in Platinum, HG and White 2 on the Fold, side by side with PokeDaisy.

**Phase 2: field 2D (core, Shadow2D).**
- Build the Repeat mode (edge carry-over) and the fog BG tile-out.
- Check the compose rules against EXPAND on the same scenes.
- The Shadow2D OnePlus stall it waited on is fixed and merged (11-17, [plan-gpu-2d-shadow.md](plan-gpu-2d-shadow.md), "Fixed").
- Size: a few hundred lines in the core.

**Phase 3: Gen 4 battles (core plus game state).**
- BG3 drawn wide, scenery sprites from OAM, and HP and text box carry with closing outlines, all switched on by the Phase 1 game state.
- Replaces plan-widemelon step 7.
- Size: about 800–1,200 lines; the largest phase.
- Phone check: HG and Platinum battle intros and switch-ins against PokeDaisy.

**Phase 4: polish and leftovers.**
- 4:3 blurred backdrop for menus, the ZOOM touch button, camera off on fault.
- Check the seam and cracks, and add `GrowPolygon` if the cracks show.
- Stage 1b if the render-width fix (see "Next") isn't enough. The fill cost has now shown up.

**Phase 5, only for the PokeDaisy swap:** WideMelon on the soft renderer at 1x.

## Decisions

- **Zoom steps.** Integer steps are pixel-exact but coarse. Our 0.25 steps are smooth but blur a little. Default: keep 0.25 and add integer steps as an option.
- **Menus.** 4:3 with a blurred backdrop, or our current 1x with black wings. Default: take the blurred backdrop for games with known game state, and leave others as they are.
- **Match PokeDaisy's camera behavior, quirks included** (user, 11-17). Where their behavior has a rough edge, we keep it and take their fix when they make one, instead of diverging. That keeps the copied code and rules diffable and the switch smooth if they adopt our core. Two known cases:
  - Interiors read as field and stay zoomed out.
  - Leaving a SoulSilver building, our zoom blipped: after the fade the scene reads as text for about 0.8 s. The side-by-side on 11-17 showed PokeDaisy (v1.5.0.1) doesn't do this, so it's fixed here (see Status).
- **Soft at 1x.** PokeDaisy runs 1x on the soft renderer. If they switch to WideMelon without it, 1x players lose widescreen unless PokeDaisy moves 1x to OpenGL (GLES 3.2). Decide before pitching, not before Phase 1.

How it works is in MODS.md, Camera, "Pokemon game state".

Left out or changed from PokeDaisy, and why:
- **No flying.** PokeDaisy's "not while flying" comes from its own FREE FLIGHT / scenic FLY feature (`pk_flight.c`, GBA games), not from the DS field read, so there's no flying flag to return to the camera or to `melon.game.state()`.
- **Battles are their own scene.** PokeDaisy's DS scene mapping returns OFF (4) for a battle; ours returns 3 (its SCENE_BATTLE_START number) so Lua can tell battles from the title. The camera treats it exactly like OFF, 0.42 s pull-in included.
- **Text after a fade waits 1.5 s** (ours only, see the fixes above).
- **The walking rules follow "Return to center when moving".** PokeDaisy always springs back while walking; ours does both walking rules (spring back, and stick-follow with the player kept inside) only with that setting on, and otherwise pans as in other games.
- **Our controls stay.** Our pan speed (300 px/s, not 150), dead zone, touch drag, pinch and the 0.25-step zoom stay. Standing with the stick uses our eased pan; the springs move the shown pan directly, as PokeDaisy's do.
- **Swapped screens and `hold_native`** still snap to 1x at once; in a known game the player's zoom then comes back like after any close-up (0.5 s, eased) rather than our 0.25 s.
- **Copied code kept whole.** The touch rules, WILD ENCOUNTERS, BGM settle and X-menu keep code in `pk_nds_field.cpp` is unused but stays, since it pulls in nothing (no `pk_nds_call`), to keep the file diffable. Nothing writes RAM: the write callback is null.
- **Two carts of our own** (`GameState.cpp`, before PokeDaisy's tables). US Platinum rev 0 (CPUE, revision byte 0) uses PokeDaisy's rev 1 table as it is: pret/pokeplatinum's checksums show both revisions share the ARM9 and every overlay but GTS. Diamond (ADAE rev 5, and rev 13, the library's European cart) has its own table from pret/pokediamond, whose US build matches the cart module for module. Both checked on the desktop build (field, text, X menu, party, a door's fade, a wild battle drawn wide; `logs/gen4-new-carts/`). Pearl isn't mapped: its ARM9 and field and battle overlays differ from Diamond's, and there's no Pearl cart to read them from.
